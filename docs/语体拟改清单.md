**为什么不能自动改**：B 级是按文本里出现的关键词归类的，含 `pirate` 不代表说话人是海盗——例如 `On behalf of my nation, I would like to thank you for destroying a convoy of the notorious pirate…` 是总督在道谢。所以自动批改会把官员的台词降成常体。

python -c "import csv,io;rows=[r for r in csv.DictReader(io.open('docs/csv/speaker-audit.csv',encoding='utf-8-sig')) if r['person']=='第1人称' and r['category'] in ('海盗/私掠','酒馆/醉汉')];print(len(rows))"
