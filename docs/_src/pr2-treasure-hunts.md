# Port Royale 2 三段式寻宝：诗谜与解法（社区汇编摘录）

- 来源：<https://steamcommunity.com/app/12470/discussions/0/1354868867712622875/>

- 原帖说明：作者称清单整理自 Selket 的收集，正文标题为「Threepart Treasure Hunt - Texts」、
  「All treasure hunt texts from Port Royale 2 and their solutions」；标 `*` 的英文文本是

  关于 30 天期限的附注）。楼层回复、图片、签名等未抄。

## 结构（判据的前提）

每条寻宝线索由三段组成：**区域**（「在东方/北方/加勒比中部…」）、**与 X 的距离**
（「离 X 不超过一天航程」）、**描述 X 的诗谜本身**。三段合起来才定位宝箱，诗谜本身
只给出 X。另：拿到第三条线索后 30 天内不取，宝箱消失。

## Hunt Text 1

The place lies somewhere in the east.
The place lies somewhere close to X, up to 1 day's journey away.
X lies on the island, it's really quite small. But look to the southeast, there's none larger at all.

Solution: X is San Juan or St. Thome

## Hunt Text 2

This place is located somewhere in the west.
The position lies in an area 1 day's journey from X.
Once long ago did other people here live, with their great temples praise to the gods did they give.
Above it lies X, up in a corner. You'll sail to the southeast in vain, I should warn you.

Solution: X is Cancun, near Sisal

## Hunt Text 3*

It is the most western town in the region.
The place lies somewhere close to X.
The place lies somewhere in the west.

Solution: X is Tampico

## Hunt Text 4

The place lies somewhere in the northwest.
The town that you seek is a desolate place, far flung from all the world, as though forsaken, or in disgrace.
The place is located somewhere to the south of X, not far from the coast.

Solution: X is Corpus Christi

## Hunt Text 5

The place is located not far from X, at most 1 day's journey away.
This must be a southerly location.
Here lies the town, overlooking the sound. It guards the bay, for with ships it abounds.

Solution: X is Maracaibo

## Hunt Text 6

This must be a position in the middle of the Caribbean.
The position is located close to X.
Three little islands all alone, but only on one you can find ropes.

Solution: X is Turk Islands

## Hunt Text 7

Not far from the town, at the bottom of a cliff, lie the battered remains of an old wrecked ship.

Solution: X is Caracas

X is a town that has streets paved with gold. Its majestic harbor is a sight to behold.
This must be a position in the middle of the Caribbean.

Solution: X is Port Royale

## Hunt Text 9

## Hunt Text 10a

An island lies here, as though in a cradle. A peninsula curls around at a angle.
The place is located to the west of X.

Solution: X is Providence

## Hunt Text 11

This must be a position in the north.
The position is located close to X.
A lonely town lies in the west of the headland.

Solution: X is Grand Bahama

The city in the west is Tampa/ Florida. The chest can also be found north of Grand Bahama behind a rock (partly covered).

## Hunt Text 21

## Hunt Text 22

The place lies in the southeast Caribbean.
Spain set up a harbor here once. You will find wood and bricks, but wheat there is none.
This place is located not far from X, at most 1 day's journey away.

Solution: X is Port of Spain

## Hunt Text 23

White strand beside the jungle, a little river known to none. Here you lie well hidden, in a bay seen only by the sun.
This must be a southerly location.
The place is located not far from X, at most 1 day's journey away.

Solution: X is Santa Marta.

## Hunt Text 24

Holy August is now northward bound, very soon in the town is where he'll be found.
The position is located 3 to 4 days' journey to the east of X.
The place must lie in the far northeast.

Solution: X is St. Augustine, next to the island to the East.

## Hunt Text 25

An island far off, small and remote. There is nowhere else for X to denote.

Solution: X is Cayman

## Hunt Text 26*

The position is located not far from X.
Here, you should go, if you would like to bake colorful, sweet corn fields with vegetables in the clay oven.
The place lies somewhere in the north.

Solution: X is Eleuthera, behind the southeastern neighboring island

## Hunt Text 27*

The place is located somewhere to the east of X, not far from the coast.
The place is located somewhere in the west.
In a large bay lie some towns, there are three. X refers to the most western of these.

Solution: X is Vera Cruz

## 对照我们的 29 组时要注意

- 原帖清单只收 27 条（含 10a），与我们的 41 条不是一一对应：`07`（西南版）、`12`（岛链缺口）、
  `25`（三镇最东）、`29`（河东入沼泽）在原帖里没有条目。
- `06` / `07` 是同一条谜面的两个版本（末句东南 / 西南相反），原帖只收东南版。
- `10` 与原帖 Hunt 10 同文但无解；`26` 对应 Hunt 10a；`20` 是 Hunt 10 的「peninsula/land」
  变体。
- 标 `*` 的条目其英文文本是整理者回译的，与我们的 `original-en`（GOG 1.1.2.3）可能不同字；
  判据以我们的原文为准，原帖只用来核对「X 是哪座城」。
